20 Perfect Date Night Movies for Two

The hardest part of date night is agreeing on the film. These are our reliable two-person crowd-pleasers — swoony, funny and conversation-sparking, with none of the awkward lulls. Each pick comes with where to stream it.

By 36VIBES Updated May 30, 2026 8 min read

The best date night movie does two jobs at once: it keeps you both entertained and it gives you something to talk about when the credits roll. We grouped these by the kind of night you are after.

❤️ Swoony & Romantic

When you want to lean in and let the butterflies do the work.

★ IMDb 7.8

About Time (2013)

A time-travel romance that is really about cherishing ordinary days. Quietly devastating in the best way. Stream on: Netflix.

★ IMDb 8.0

Before Sunrise (1995)

Two strangers, one night in Vienna, endless conversation. The ultimate talk-and-fall-in-love film. Stream on: Max.

😄 Funny & Easy

Low risk, high charm — perfect for a first or fiftieth date.

★ IMDb 7.4

Crazy Rich Asians (2018)

Glossy, funny and gorgeous to look at. A feel-good rom-com with real stakes. Stream on: Max.

★ IMDb 7.4

Palm Springs (2020)

A time-loop comedy with genuine heart and chemistry. Smart, sweet and rewatchable. Stream on: Hulu.

🍷 A Little Spark

For when you want sexy, stylish or a touch dramatic.

★ IMDb 8.1

In the Mood for Love (2000)

Achingly beautiful and full of longing. A film you will both be thinking about for days. Stream on: Max.

★ IMDb 8.0

La La Land (2016)

Dreamy, bittersweet and built for two. Leaves you floating even as it breaks your heart a little. Stream on: Netflix.

How we pick date night films

Every 36VIBES list is scored on our internal vibe scale. For date night, we weight three things: how easy it is for two people to agree on, how much it sparks conversation, and whether it keeps the mood warm rather than heavy. We skip anything too bleak or too divisive.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a good date night movie?

A great date night movie is easy to agree on, sparks conversation, and is not so heavy that it kills the mood. Romance, smart comedy, and a touch of escapism all work well for two.

What is a safe date night movie pick for a first date?

Lean funny and warm rather than intense. Crowd-pleasers like About Time, Crazy Rich Asians, or Palm Springs are charming, low-risk, and give you plenty to talk about afterward.
