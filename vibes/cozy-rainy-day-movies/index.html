21 Cozy Movies for a Rainy Day That Feel Like a Warm Hug

Grey skies, the sound of rain on the window, and absolutely nowhere to be. These are the comfort films we reach for when we want to feel cocooned — each one matched to the perfect cozy mood, with where to stream it.

By 36VIBES Updated May 30, 2026 8 min read

There's a specific kind of magic to a rainy day spent indoors. The world slows down, the lighting goes soft, and the only real decision is what to watch. The problem? Scrolling through endless thumbnails kills the vibe before the movie even starts.

So we did the work for you. Below are 21 of our favorite cozy films — the kind with gentle pacing, warm color palettes, and endings that leave you feeling a little lighter. We've grouped them by the exact mood you're in.

How we choose our cozy picks

Every film on a 36VIBES list is rated on our "cozy scale" — measuring tone, visual warmth, stakes, and how satisfying the ending feels. We prioritize films that ask nothing of you except to relax. No homework, no devastating twists, no cliffhangers.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a movie good for a rainy day?

Cozy rainy-day movies usually share a warm, low-stakes tone, comforting visuals, and a satisfying ending. Gentle pacing, found-family stories, and a feel-good resolution are the common ingredients.

Where can I stream these cozy movies?

Most picks here are on Netflix, Prime Video, Max, or Disney+. We note the platform next to each film, though availability can shift by region and over time.
