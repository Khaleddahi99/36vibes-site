Prime Video Guide

The Best Movies on Prime Video Right Now, by Mood

Prime Video buries great films under rental upsells and a messy interface. So we pulled the genuinely-worth-it titles included with your membership and sorted them by exactly how you feel tonight — updated for 2026, with a one-line reason for each.

By 36VIBES Updated May 30, 2026 7 min read

Availability shifts month to month and by country, and Prime loves to nudge you toward paid rentals — so double-check the "included with Prime" tag before you settle in. Pick your vibe and skip the scroll.

☔ Cozy & Comfort

★ IMDb 7.5

The Big Sick (2017)

A warm, funny, true-story romance that handles heartbreak with real tenderness.

★ IMDb 7.8

The Grand Budapest Hotel (2014)

Wes Anderson at his most cozy and candy-colored. A perfect rewatch comfort blanket.

☀️ Feel-Good

★ IMDb 7.8

Little Miss Sunshine (2006)

A dysfunctional family road trip that ends in pure, ridiculous joy.

★ IMDb 7.7

The Intouchables (2011)

An odd-couple friendship that is impossible to watch without smiling.

🧠 Mind-Bending

★ IMDb 8.1

The Prestige (2006)

Two rival magicians and a puzzle that rewards every rewatch. Pay attention.

🔥 Adrenaline

★ IMDb 7.6

Sicario (2015)

A taut, dread-soaked border thriller with some of the tensest set pieces going.

😂 Funny

★ IMDb 7.3

Borat (2006)

Still one of the boldest comedies ever made. Wince, gasp, laugh, repeat.
