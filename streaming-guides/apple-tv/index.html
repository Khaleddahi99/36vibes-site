Apple TV+ Guide

The Best Movies on Apple TV+ Right Now, by Mood

Apple TV+ keeps a small but unusually polished catalog — prestige originals and the occasional awards heavyweight. We sorted the best of it by exactly how you feel tonight, updated for 2026, with a one-line reason for each.

By 36VIBES Updated May 30, 2026 6 min read

The library is lean, so quality stays high. Pick your vibe and skip the scroll.

☔ Cozy & Comfort

★ IMDb 7.4

CODA (2021)

A warm, funny coming-of-age story that earned its Best Picture win. Pure heart.

★ IMDb 6.9

The Family Stone (2005)

A messy, cozy holiday ensemble that is comfort-watch catnip on a cold night.

☀️ Feel-Good

★ IMDb 7.5

Cha Cha Real Smooth (2022)

A gentle, big-hearted story about figuring life out. Quietly uplifting.

★ IMDb 6.8

Spirited (2022)

A splashy musical spin on A Christmas Carol that leans into pure joy.

🧠 Mind-Bending

★ IMDb 7.2

Tetris (2023)

A surprisingly tense Cold War thriller about the fight over a video game. Wild and twisty.

🔥 Adrenaline

★ IMDb 7.6

Greyhound (2020)

A lean, relentless WWII naval thriller. Ninety minutes of pure tension.

🎥 Awards Heavyweights

★ IMDb 7.6

Killers of the Flower Moon (2023)

Scorsese's sweeping, devastating epic. A demanding but unforgettable watch.
