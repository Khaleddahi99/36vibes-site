Netflix Guide

The Best Movies on Netflix Right Now, by Mood

Netflix's library is huge and the menu is useless. So we sorted the genuinely-worth-it films by exactly how you feel tonight — updated for 2026, with a one-line reason for each.

By 36VIBES Updated May 30, 2026 7 min read

Availability shifts month to month and by country, so we re-check this list regularly. Pick your vibe and skip the scroll.

☔ Cozy & Comfort

★ IMDb 7.3

Chef (2014)

A warm, food-filled reset about loving what you do. The ultimate low-stakes comfort watch.

★ IMDb 7.8

Pride & Prejudice (2005)

Misty mornings, longing glances, and the coziest period romance going.

☀️ Feel-Good

★ IMDb 8.0

Sing Street (2016)

A teen starts a band to impress a girl — pure, joyful, 80s-soundtracked magic.

★ IMDb 8.0

La La Land (2016)

Dreamy and bittersweet — leaves you floating even as it breaks your heart a little.

🧠 Mind-Bending

★ IMDb 8.8

Inception (2010)

Dreams within dreams, built like a heist. The gold standard of the genre.

🔥 Adrenaline

★ IMDb 7.4

John Wick (2014)

Stylish, kinetic, and relentless. Switch your brain off and enjoy the choreography.

😂 Funny

★ IMDb 7.6

Superbad (2007)

Chaotic, quotable teen comedy that still holds up. Comfort laughs guaranteed.
