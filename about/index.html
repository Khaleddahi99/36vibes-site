Our Story

We turn "I don't know what to watch" into "let's watch this."

36VIBES exists for one reason: choosing a movie should take 30 seconds, not 30 minutes. We organize films by how they make you feel — because that's actually how people decide.

Why mood, not genre?

Genre tells you what a film is. Mood tells you what it'll do for you. "Action" doesn't help when what you really mean is "I had a brutal day and need to switch my brain off." We map every film to the feeling it delivers, so the right pick finds you faster.

How we curate every pick

This is the part we take seriously. A film only makes a 36VIBES list if it clears three checks:

1. Critics rated it

We start with films that earned strong reviews — a baseline of quality so we're never recommending something hollow.

2. Audiences loved it

Critic darlings that real viewers bounced off don't make the cut. We weigh audience scores just as heavily.

3. A human matched the vibe

Then a real person — not an algorithm — confirms the film actually feels like the mood it's filed under. This is where most recommendation sites stop short, and where we don't.

The "vibe scale"

Every pick gets a mood-specific score. A cozy film is rated on warmth and comfort; a mind-bender on how cleverly it hides its logic. Same rigor, tuned to what each mood is actually for.

How we make money (transparency)

36VIBES is free. We earn through display ads and, occasionally, affiliate links to streaming services — at no extra cost to you. It never influences which films we recommend. If a pick is on the list, it's because it earned its place. See our affiliate disclosure.
