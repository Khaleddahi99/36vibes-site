Sci-Fi Guide

The Best Sci-Fi Movies of All Time, by Vibe

Science fiction can blow your mind, break your heart or simply blow up a planet. We sorted the all-time greats by the exact vibe you want tonight, with a one-line reason for each.

By 36VIBES Updated May 30, 2026 8 min read

From cerebral puzzles to pure spectacle, sci-fi has a flavor for every night. Pick your vibe and skip the scroll.

🧠 Mind-Bending

★ IMDb 8.8

Inception (2010)

Dreams within dreams, built like a heist. The gold standard of the genre.

★ IMDb 8.0

Arrival (2016)

A first-contact story that is really about language, time and grief. Quietly profound.

🚀 Epic Space Opera

★ IMDb 8.7

Interstellar (2014)

Big ideas, bigger emotions and a black hole that doubles as a clock. Awe on a grand scale.

★ IMDb 8.0

Dune (2021)

Gorgeous, monumental world-building. The most immersive sandbox sci-fi in years.

🌙 Dystopian & Noir

★ IMDb 8.0

Blade Runner (1982)

Rain, neon and existential dread. The blueprint for every sci-fi noir since.

🤖 Cerebral & Intimate

★ IMDb 7.7

Ex Machina (2014)

A tense, talky chamber piece about AI and manipulation. Smart and unnerving.
