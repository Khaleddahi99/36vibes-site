Romance Guide

The Best Romance Movies to Watch

From butterflies to heartbreak, romance comes in many shades. We sorted the genuinely great love stories by the exact feeling you are chasing tonight, updated for 2026, with a one-line reason for each.

By 36VIBES Updated May 30, 2026 8 min read

Whether you want swoon, sob or a smile, there is a love story for it. Pick your mood and skip the scroll.

❤️ Swoony & Romantic

★ IMDb 8.1

Pride & Prejudice (2005)

Misty mornings, longing glances and the coziest period romance going.

★ IMDb 8.0

Before Sunrise (1995)

Two strangers, one night in Vienna and endless conversation. Talk-and-fall-in-love perfection.

💕 Bittersweet

★ IMDb 8.3

Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind (2004)

A heartbreak you would choose to keep. Inventive, aching and unforgettable.

★ IMDb 8.0

La La Land (2016)

Dreamy and bittersweet — leaves you floating even as it breaks your heart a little.

😄 Funny & Charming

★ IMDb 7.7

When Harry Met Sally (1989)

The rom-com gold standard. Sharp, warm and impossibly quotable.

🌋 Epic & Sweeping

★ IMDb 8.1

In the Mood for Love (2000)

Achingly beautiful and full of longing. A masterclass in restraint and desire.
