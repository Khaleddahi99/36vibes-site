Animation Guide

The Best Animated Movies for Grown-Ups (and Kids)

Animation is not a genre, it is a medium — and some of the most emotional, beautiful and daring films ever made are animated. We sorted the best by mood, with a one-line reason for each.

By 36VIBES Updated May 30, 2026 7 min read

From family-friendly joy to grown-up gut-punches, animation does it all. Pick your vibe and skip the scroll.

😭 Emotional Gut-Punches

★ IMDb 8.2

Up (2009)

That opening montage alone earns its place. Tender, joyful and quietly profound.

★ IMDb 8.5

Grave of the Fireflies (1988)

One of the most beautiful and heartbreaking films ever made, animated or not.

🎨 Visually Stunning

★ IMDb 8.4

Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018)

A comic book come alive. The most inventive animation of the decade.

★ IMDb 8.6

Spirited Away (2001)

A gorgeous, gentle Ghibli dream that feels like being wrapped in a warm blanket.

😂 Funny for All Ages

★ IMDb 8.3

Toy Story (1995)

Still razor-sharp and funny for every age. The film that started Pixar's run.

🌙 Dark & Daring

★ IMDb 7.9

Persepolis (2007)

A striking black-and-white memoir of growing up during the Iranian revolution.
