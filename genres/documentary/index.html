Documentary Guide

The Best Documentaries That Grip Like Fiction

The truth really is stranger than fiction — and these documentaries play like the best thrillers, dramas and mysteries. We sorted them by mood, with a one-line reason for each.

By 36VIBES Updated May 30, 2026 7 min read

Forget dry and dusty — the docs below are as gripping as any scripted film. Pick your vibe and skip the scroll.

🕸 True Crime

★ IMDb 8.3

The Act of Killing (2012)

A staggering, surreal confrontation with mass murderers. Unlike anything you have seen.

★ IMDb 7.7

The Imposter (2012)

A stranger-than-fiction tale of identity theft that keeps pulling the rug out.

🎵 Music & Culture

★ IMDb 8.0

20 Feet from Stardom (2013)

The unsung backup singers behind the biggest hits. Joyful and moving.

★ IMDb 8.1

Searching for Sugar Man (2012)

A musical mystery with a payoff that feels too good to be true. Pure magic.

🌍 Nature & Wonder

★ IMDb 8.1

My Octopus Teacher (2020)

An unexpectedly profound friendship with a sea creature. Beautiful and tender.
