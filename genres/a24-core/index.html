A24 Guide

The Best A24 Movies, Ranked by Vibe

A24 has become a vibe unto itself — surreal, stylish and unafraid to get weird. We ranked the studio's best by the exact mood they conjure, with a one-line reason for each.

By 36VIBES Updated May 30, 2026 8 min read

Whether you want a good cry, a good scream or a beautiful headscratch, A24 has it. Pick your vibe and skip the scroll.

🌌 Surreal & Mind-Expanding

★ IMDb 7.8

Everything Everywhere All at Once (2022)

A multiverse epic that is somehow also the year's most moving family drama.

★ IMDb 6.8

Under the Skin (2013)

Hypnotic, eerie and unlike anything else. Pure mood, minimal explanation.

💔 Indie Heartbreak

★ IMDb 7.4

Aftersun (2022)

A quietly shattering memory piece about a father and daughter. Devastating restraint.

★ IMDb 7.8

Lady Bird (2017)

A wry, tender coming-of-age that nails the ache of leaving home.

👻 Elevated Horror

★ IMDb 7.5

Hereditary (2018)

A grief-soaked nightmare that tightens like a vise. Genuinely upsetting in the best way.

🎭 Stylish & Bold

★ IMDb 7.6

Uncut Gems (2019)

A panic attack of a movie. Relentless, anxious and weirdly exhilarating.
