Comedy Guide

The Best Comedy Movies to Watch Right Now

A good laugh fixes almost everything. We sorted the funniest films by the exact kind of humor you are after tonight — quotable, feel-good, dark or romantic — with a one-line reason for each.

By 36VIBES Updated May 30, 2026 7 min read

Comedy is personal, so we split it by flavor. Pick your laugh and skip the scroll.

😂 Quotable Classics

★ IMDb 8.2

The Big Lebowski (1998)

The Coens' shaggy-dog masterpiece. Endlessly quotable and impossibly cool.

★ IMDb 8.1

Groundhog Day (1993)

A time-loop comedy with a soul. Funny, philosophical and perfectly built.

🎉 Feel-Good Laughs

★ IMDb 7.6

Superbad (2007)

Chaotic, quotable teen comedy that still holds up. Comfort laughs guaranteed.

★ IMDb 7.8

School of Rock (2003)

Jack Black at his most lovable. You will air-guitar by the end.

🏻 Dark & Sharp

★ IMDb 7.7

In Bruges (2008)

Hitmen, guilt and gallows humor in a fairy-tale city. Hilarious and surprisingly moving.

❤️ Rom-Com Gold

★ IMDb 7.7

When Harry Met Sally (1989)

The rom-com gold standard. Sharp, warm and impossibly quotable.
