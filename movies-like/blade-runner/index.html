15 Movies Like Blade Runner (Neon Sci-Fi Noir)

If you love rain-slicked streets, replicant dread and that aching question of what makes us human, this list is for you. We gathered the best neon-soaked sci-fi noir to fill the Blade Runner-shaped hole, with a one-line reason for each.

By 36VIBES Updated May 30, 2026 8 min read

Blade Runner basically invented a whole aesthetic. These picks chase its mood, its questions or its look — grouped by what you loved most.

🅍 Pure Neon Noir

★ IMDb 8.0

Blade Runner 2049 (2017)

The rare sequel that honors and expands the original. Stunning, melancholy and vast.

★ IMDb 7.5

Ghost in the Shell (1995)

The anime landmark that shares Blade Runner's soul — identity, machines and rain.

🤖 What Makes Us Human

★ IMDb 7.7

Ex Machina (2014)

A tense, intimate AI puzzle box. The questions Blade Runner asks, in close-up.

★ IMDb 8.0

Her (2013)

A tender, melancholy love story between a man and an AI. Quiet and profound.

🌙 Dystopian Dread

★ IMDb 8.4

Children of Men (2006)

A grimy, gripping vision of collapse with some of the best long takes ever filmed.

★ IMDb 7.6

Minority Report (2002)

Sleek future-noir built on a Philip K. Dick story, just like Blade Runner.
