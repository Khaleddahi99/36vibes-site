12 Movies Like La La Land (Dreamy Bittersweet Romance)

If you fell for the soaring songs, the golden light and that gut-punch ending, here is where to go next. We gathered the dreamiest, most bittersweet romances and musicals about love and ambition, with a one-line reason for each.

By 36VIBES Updated May 30, 2026 7 min read

La La Land blends the joy of a classic musical with a very modern ache. These picks chase one or both of those feelings, grouped by what you loved most.

🎵 Gorgeous Musicals

★ IMDb 7.8

The Umbrellas of Cherbourg (1964)

A sung-through romance in candy colors with a bittersweet heart. La La Land's clear ancestor.

★ IMDb 8.0

Sing Street (2016)

A teen starts a band to impress a girl — pure, joyful, 80s-soundtracked magic.

💕 Bittersweet Romance

★ IMDb 8.3

Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind (2004)

A heartbreak you would choose to keep. Inventive, aching and unforgettable.

★ IMDb 8.1

In the Mood for Love (2000)

Achingly beautiful and full of longing. A masterclass in restraint and desire.

🎭 Love & Ambition

★ IMDb 8.5

Whiplash (2014)

Damien Chazelle's other masterpiece. The cost of chasing greatness, in overdrive.

★ IMDb 7.4

Begin Again (2013)

A warm, music-filled story about second chances and creative sparks in the city.
