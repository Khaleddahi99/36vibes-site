14 Movies Like Parasite (Sharp, Twisty Thrillers)

If you loved the way Parasite mixed pitch-black comedy, class warfare and a mid-film swerve you never saw coming, this list is your next obsession. We gathered the sharpest, twistiest thrillers around, with a one-line reason for each.
